February 2026 availability report

We are pleased to report on Acme Payments service availability for February 2026.

Service availability

  • payments-api: 99.99%
  • merchant-portal: 99.97%

Public incidents

We experienced 2 public incidents during February 2026.

On February 9, merchants experienced delayed webhooks as we worked to resolve an issue with our webhook certificate. Webhook delivery resumed to normal latency once the certificate was renewed.

On February 24, merchants reported slow performance when exporting reports from the merchant portal. We identified and resolved the underlying cause, and report export performance returned to normal.

Looking ahead

We remain committed to maintaining high availability and reliability for our merchant partners. We continue to monitor our systems closely and work to minimize disruptions to payment processing and merchant operations.
