Status: card payments failing

Document: STS-301

Date: 2026-03-12

Services: payments-api

Investigating

We are investigating an issue affecting card payments. The problem began at 14:15 UTC. We posted this update at 14:20 UTC.

Identified

At 14:35 UTC we identified the root cause: a third-party card network experienced a service disruption that prevented card authorizations from completing. This affected fewer than 300 merchants.

Monitoring

We are monitoring the situation and working with our card network partner to confirm full recovery.

Resolved

Service fully recovered at 15:10 UTC. All merchants can now process card payments normally.
