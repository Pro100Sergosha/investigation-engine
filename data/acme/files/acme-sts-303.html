Status: 3-D Secure verification failures for Visa cards

Document: STS-303

Date: 2026-03-26

Services: card-gateway

Investigating

We identified an issue affecting 3-D Secure verification for Visa cardholders. Some customers attempting to complete 3-D Secure verification were unable to proceed with their transactions. Our card-gateway service was operating normally, and the issue was traced to our acquiring partner.

Identified

The root cause was determined to be on the side of our acquiring partner. We worked with them to resolve the verification failures affecting Visa cards.

Resolved

The issue has been Resolved. 3-D Secure verification for Visa cardholders is now functioning normally. Merchants should see transactions processing as expected.

If you continue to experience issues with 3-D Secure verification, please contact our support team.
