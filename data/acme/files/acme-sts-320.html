March 2026 availability report

We are publishing our monthly availability report for March 2026. During this period, we experienced 2 public incidents that affected merchant services.

Service availability

  • payments-api: 99.82%
  • merchant-portal: 99.98%

Incidents

On March 12, 2026, merchants experienced a service disruption affecting card payment processing. The payments-api service was unavailable during this incident, preventing merchants from accepting payments. The issue was identified and resolved, and we have since implemented additional monitoring and safeguards to prevent recurrence.

On March 26, 2026, merchants encountered failures when processing card payments that required 3-D Secure authentication. This incident affected the card authorization flow for a subset of transactions. The issue was resolved, and we continue to work with our acquiring bank partner NorthBank to strengthen the reliability of 3-D Secure processing.

Commitment

We recognize that payment processing reliability is critical to your business. We are committed to maintaining high availability and transparency about incidents affecting our services. Our teams continue to invest in infrastructure resilience, monitoring, and incident response capabilities.

For detailed incident reports, please contact our support team.
