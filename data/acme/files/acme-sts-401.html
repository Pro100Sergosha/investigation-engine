Status: merchant portal login issues

Document: STS-401

Date: 2026-04-07

Services: merchant-portal

Investigating

At 2026-04-07 14:32 UTC, merchants reported being unable to log in to the merchant portal. Our team began investigating immediately.

During this time, the payments API and payments were not affected. Merchants who were already logged in could continue to use the portal normally.

Identified

We identified an issue with the merchant portal authentication system. The login service was experiencing elevated error rates that prevented new sessions from being established.

Resolved

The issue was resolved at 2026-04-07 15:17 UTC. Merchants are now able to log in to the merchant portal without difficulty.

The incident lasted about 45 minutes. We apologize for the disruption and thank you for your patience while we worked to restore service.
