Status: merchant portal login issues

Document: STS-401

Date: 2026-04-07

Services: merchant-portal

Investigating

At 08:10 UTC on April 7, 2026, we identified that merchants were unable to log in to the merchant portal. We immediately began investigating the issue.

During this incident, the API and payments were not affected. Merchants could continue to process payments normally through our REST API.

Resolved

At 08:50 UTC, we resolved the login issue. The merchant portal is now fully operational and merchants can access their accounts normally.

The incident lasted about 45 minutes. We apologize for any disruption this caused to your access to payment reports and settings during this time.
