Status: elevated card declines

Document: STS-101

Date: 2026-01-21

Services: payments-api, fraud-scorer

Investigating

At 10:32 UTC on January 21, 2026, we identified an elevated rate of card payment declines affecting the payments-api and fraud-scorer services. Legitimate customer payments were being declined at higher than normal rates. Our teams began immediate investigation to identify the cause and restore normal service.

Identified

We identified a faulty risk model update in the fraud-scorer service that was incorrectly flagging legitimate transactions as high-risk. The decline rate peaked at 6.5% during the incident window. Once identified, we began rolling back the problematic update to restore normal fraud scoring behavior.

Resolved

At 11:45 UTC, we completed the rollback and confirmed that card payment declines had returned to baseline rates. The payments-api and fraud-scorer services are operating normally. All legitimate payment requests are being processed without the elevated decline rates experienced during the incident.

We apologize for the disruption to your payment processing. If you have questions about payments declined during this window, please contact our support team.
